Accelerated Bachelors/Masters
Programs in which students share limited credits between their
undergraduate and graduate degrees to allow completion of both
degrees. Such programs are also known as 4+1 Bachelor’s/Master’s
programs, 5 Year Programs, 5-year dual degree programs, or Combined
Bachelor’s/Master’s programs. 
Certiﬁcate
An organized program of study that requires a minimum of 8 credit hours.
Certiﬁcates exist at three levels: bachelor’s, post-baccalaureate, and
graduate.  Graduate certiﬁcate students must have earned a bachelor’s
degree. Bachelor’s certiﬁcates are comprised of courses ranging from
100-300 level. Post-baccalaureate certiﬁcates are comprised of courses
between 100-400 level and graduate certiﬁcates must be comprised
entirely of courses at the 400 level or above.
Concentration
A concentration is a more focused area of study most typically within
an undergraduate major. The minimum credit hour requirement for a
concentration is 9 hours.
Double Major
Undergraduate students may complete more than one Bachelor of Arts
major, more than one Bachelor of Science major and more than one
Bachelor of Business Administration major from the same school within
120 hours. Students must complete the Core requirements and the
requirements of all academic majors.  One diploma is issued.
Dual Degree
Loyola offers dual degree programs leading to two master’s degrees
(e.g., MDiv/MSW) or to a graduate and ﬁrst professional degree (e.g.,
MA/JD). The requirements for each of these dual degree programs vary.
Two separate diplomas are issued when students complete two distinct
degrees from different schools within Loyola University Chicago.
Endorsement
The endorsement is an ISBE (Illinois State Board of Education) credential.
The courses in these programs allow the student a professional pathway
to career paths in education.
Major
That component of the undergraduate curriculum consisting of a set of
courses selected and sequenced to provide students with the opportunity
for extensive and in-depth study of a discipline or interdisciplinary ﬁeld of
study. Requirements for the major may also include integrating elements
or experiences, such as a senior thesis or capstone course. The minimum
number of hours required for a major is 30 credit hours. A major is
required for all bachelor’s degrees.
Minor
At the undergraduate level, a prescribed grouping of courses in a school,
department, or interdisciplinary program, more than 50% of which are
usually in upper division courses. The minimum number of hours required
for a minor is 18 credits. A minor is not required.  At the graduate level,
see entry for “specialization. ”

Pre-Major Coursework
At the Associate Degree level, a minimum of 6 credit hours that may
constitute credit toward a deﬁned major in a future bachelor’s degree.
Track/Specialization
An integrated, coherent set of courses that deﬁne a limited topic or ﬁeld
of study, used most often at the graduate level as part of an academic
degree program. Specializations may be prescribed by a department/
program or constructed by students in consultation with an advisor. A
minimum of 9 credit hours in the program of which it is a part, with the
remaining hours taken in common by all students in the program.
